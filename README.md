# High-Res Business Card Builder

A professional, vector-grade print-ready business card designer. Built to help owners, designers, and teams craft highly accurate business card designs conforming to actual global printing industry standards.

## Key Features
- **Industry Presets**: Quick switching between UK/EU Standard (85x55mm) and US Standard (3.5" x 2.0") form factors.
- **Visual Print Guides**: Real-time display of **safe margins**, **trim crop guides**, and **bleed offsets** (3.2mm / 0.125" standard bounds).
- **Interactive Layers**: Drag, resize, configure depth (z-index), rotate, and modify text/SVGs/images on the fly.
- **CMYK Simulation**: Built-in color controller converting standard HEX to printable CMYK parameters (C, M, Y, K percentages) for print accuracy.
- **AI Layout Suggestions**: Powered by server-side Gemini AI models that recommend perfect palettes, modern structures, and tailored copy.
- **Print Template Prep**: High-fidelity export including professional crop/trim marks and registration color bars, ready for physical printing.
